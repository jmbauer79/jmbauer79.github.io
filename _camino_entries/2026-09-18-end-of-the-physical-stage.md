    Afterwards I have a nearly 20-mile walk to Hontanas. This starts the second phase of my Camino journey, traditionally known as the mental/emotional phase.


    It’s also when I start the Meseta, a long section of large flat plains in the centre of Spain, between the cities of Burgos and Leon. Some pilgrims actually skip this part. Others find it to be fundamentally necessary to the Camino journey. Time will tell what I encounter.
